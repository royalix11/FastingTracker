# App Naming Research Report
**Date:** July 2026
**Project:** Premium Fasting Tracker (Black & Neon Theme)

---

## Executive Summary
This naming strategy and market research report establishes a distinctive, commanding brand identity for our sleek, minimal Android fasting tracker. While existing competitors lean heavily into generic descriptive labels (*Intermittent Fasting Tracker, Fasting App*) or casual names (*Zero, Simple, Fastic*), our app embraces an **Obsidian Flux & Technological Stoicism** aesthetic: pitch-black `#0F0E0A` surfaces, electric `#FFDE4D` neon yellow/orange accents, geometric precision, and cyber-minimalist **Geist** typography.

To reinforce this visual identity, we performed live search verification across the Google Play Store, Apple App Store, and major trademark/domain databases. Each recommended name evokes precision, metabolic mastery, circadian rhythmicity, and sleek cybernetic focus while remaining **100% distinct** from major existing health apps.

## Next Steps / Selection Framework

To select the final production brand name from the top recommendations above, use this 3-tier evaluation framework:

1. **Brand Dominance & Recall (Recommended Winner: `Obsidian Fast` or `Void Fast`)**
   * **Why `Obsidian Fast`:** Unmistakable alignment with our core user rule (*Obsidian Flux theme*). Immediately communicates an ultra-dark OLED aesthetic that sets us apart from white/pastel competitors.
   * **Why `Void Fast`:** Communicates radical simplicity and zero bloat/ads.

2. **App Store Optimization (ASO) Pairing**
   * Structure the Google Play title as: **`[Brand Name] - Intermittent Fasting Tracker`** (e.g., *`Obsidian Fast - Minimal Fasting Tracker`* or *`Void Fast - Dark Fasting Timer`*). This captures both distinctive brand identity and high-volume organic ASO keywords.

3. **Visual Iconography & Package Naming**
   * Ensure the Android applicationId reflects the selected name cleanly (e.g., `com.obsidianflux.fasting` or `com.voidfast.app`).
